# Beathill Studio

## Short description

Create polished short videos, then export free or publish with Pro.

## Full description

Beathill Studio turns selected video clips into finished short-form videos.

Upload one or more clips from your phone and let processing continue in the
background. Review the generated video, choose a thumbnail, edit titles and
descriptions, download the finished files, and publish to connected platforms
when those integrations are configured.

Features:

- Resumable background video uploads
- Multi-clip video creation
- Automatic transcription and caption generation
- Thumbnail selection and text editing
- Generated titles and descriptions
- Video preview and file downloads
- Pro publishing to configured social platforms
- Separate YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok connections
- Google account recovery and quick account switching
- Light, dark, and device theme modes

Free users can create and export videos. Direct publishing requires an active
Beathill Studio Pro subscription and configured platform connections.
