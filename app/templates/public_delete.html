Delete your Beathill Studio account and data

Beathill Studio (operated by Beathill Racing) lets you permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time.

Delete from inside the app

  • Open Beathill Studio and go to the Settings tab.
  • In the Account section, tap Delete account and data.
  • Confirm. Your account and data are removed immediately.

Delete by email

If you cannot access the app, email info@beathillracing.fi from the address linked to your account and request deletion. Requests are completed within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and installation / login identifiers.
  • Uploaded and generated videos, thumbnails, captions and publishing metadata.
  • Linked Google profile data (email, name) and connected social-platform tokens.
  • Usage records associated with your account.

What may be retained

  • Google Play purchase records required for billing, tax and fraud-prevention, retained only as long as required by law.
  • Minimal technical logs kept briefly for security, then deleted.

Subscriptions

Deleting your app data does not cancel an active Beathill Studio Pro subscription. Manage or cancel it in your Google Play subscriptions.
